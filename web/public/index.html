Data comes from NSE: daily bulk and block deals (trades of 0.5% or more of a company), insider trades by promoters and directors (SEBI PIT disclosures), large-stake filings (SEBI SAST: crossing 5% or moving 2% or more), and each company's quarterly shareholding filing (every holder with 1% or more). It is checked automatically every 30 minutes on weekdays and appears here within about an hour of NSE publishing it. Trading firms that buy and sell the same stock on the same day are left out.
